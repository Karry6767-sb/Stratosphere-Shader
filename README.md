## 2.Highlight
This shader combines strengths from many mainstream shaders, while also featuring several original implementations.
<img width="1280" height="720" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1fde130f-c58c-4486-9882-35f75968d357" />

The shader is still under active development, and more improvements are planned for future updates.
I implemented Ray-Marching to render comets in the sky. You can see Halley's Comet up to 30 years ahead of its real-world arrival.
There is also a chance for random meteors to spawn, and meteor showers will appear at specific times.
<img width="1280" height="720" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/5177390b-5d07-4c09-8381-2b31fcd042fe" />

This shader uses NASA lunar textures. It includes a moon phase system, and the moon will affect the in-game environment.
<img width="1280" height="720" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f8d6120d-5727-4a23-bead-f2b92ba3dcbf" />

> I tried my best to implement an independent lunar orbit and build a physically-based sky system.
