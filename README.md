## 2.Highlight
This shader combines the advantages of many mainstream shaders. At the same time, there are also parts that are original.
<img width="1280" height="720" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1fde130f-c58c-4486-9882-35f75968d357" />
The shader is still in the development stage, and the effects may be better in the future.

Also, I introduced the concept of Ray-March, causing comets to appear in the sky, allowing us to see Halley's Comet as much as 30 years in advance.
And there is also a chance for meteors to appear in the sky, and even meteor showers can occur at specific times.
